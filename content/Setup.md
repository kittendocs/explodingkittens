1. Separate all exploding kittens (6 in total) and put those aside.
2. Separate all [[Defuse]] cards (there are 4) and deal one to each player. Shuffle 2 defuses in the deck. (In case there are too few, shuffle as many as you can in.)
3. Shuffle the deck.
4. Deal 7 cards to each player. Everyone should have 8 cards now.
5. Shuffle the deck and place it face down on the table. 
6. Pick a player to go first. You may choose any criteria you like.

> [!WARNING] For the sake of ageism, please don't do youngest player goes first (unless there is an exploding kitten at the top of the deck)
